# Shree Maruti Transport Services — A country in motion

## Edition

Branch: `codex/visual-elite`. This is a separate design proposal based on the latest working website content, not a change to main. Company facts remain: established 2001, 50+ owned tankers, Bhiwandi base, pan-India operations, 16/21/25/30/35 MT capacities, SS 304/316/316L and food-grade liquid transport.

## Visual identity

Midnight green, soft ivory and acid citrus; Outfit typography; asymmetric photo-led hero; expansive chapter spacing.

## Layout and interaction

GSAP image scale/fade scrubbing, overlapping pinned cards, route-entry reveals, layered hero photo/text parallax, horizontal service accordions, interactive India route selection, safety consideration selector, customer-perspective carousel and a continuous client marquee.

All pages retain navigation, substantive service/company material and an enquiry path. Eleven routes include the original seven pages, a dedicated network page, and three detailed service guides. The enquiry form still prepares a local draft. Indexing remains off throughout the client preview. No domain-dependent canonical URLs or new phone/email details are invented.

## References and decisions

- Visual edition: user-specified `gpt-taste` skill. Deterministic seed 439 selected Artistic Asymmetry, Outfit, infinite marquee, horizontal accordions, customer-perspective carousel, card stacking and image scale/fade. The carousel presents practical buyer considerations rather than invented attributed testimonials.
- Simpler edition: [SwiftMove by Sahin Mia / Pixelean](https://dribbble.com/shots/26866271-SwiftMove-Logistics-Transportation-Website-Design), inspected for structure. The site uses original Shree Maruti content and supplied/generated fleet assets, not copied showcase assets.
- Both editions: [Osmo parallax reference on 21st](https://21st.dev/community/components/s/hero?preview=%2F%40osmosupply%2Fcomponents%2Fparallax-scrolling), inspected for foreground/text/background depth. This is scroll-responsive parallax, not a simulated driving video.
- Existing client-requested blue-and-white Tata imagery is retained. No generic unrelated stock imagery replaces the fleet.

## Accessibility and content boundaries

Reduced-motion settings disable animated effects. Core content remains visible without animation. Navigation, city selectors, accordions and safety selectors use native links/buttons. Continuous client marquees have hover/focus pause and reduced-motion alternatives, with no visible pause button per client direction. The full client list remains in the page markup.

Route lines are geographic planning corridors, not a live fleet tracker or a list of confirmed branches. Proposed transport scenarios and operating descriptions remain client-meeting copy to verify before launch. No visible draft labels are shown, as requested. Company certification statements come from the user; no extra certifications or performance statistics have been added.

# Homepage road animation

The homepage uses a browser animation, without a video service or runtime media API. Its camera angle follows the client's `~/Downloads/shree_maruti_transport_images/header.png`: both the front of the cab and the side of the tanker remain visible.

## Assets

- `public/hero/tanker.webp`: 1536 × 1024, generated from the supplied header image with the built-in image generator. Preserves the blue-and-white Tata cab, decorated signage, tank stripes, garland and three-quarter view. The generator returned an opaque background; `TankerCutout` clips the photograph to the vehicle silhouette in the browser. Four SVG rims use fixed metal shading with rotating vents and lug details inside elliptical perspective transforms. The photographic tyres remain unchanged.
- `public/hero/plant-perspective.webp`: 1774 × 887, generated by removing the tanker from the supplied `header.png` and extending its industrial surroundings. The source is levelled and cropped above its photographed kerb. Two mirrored panels form each repeat; two repeats fill the moving strip. The plant and the separately drawn continuous road share one angled plane.
- `public/hero/asphalt-grain.svg`: seamless procedural grain for the road surface, independent of the plant photograph's lighting and perspective.

Both generated PNG originals remain under `/home/gaurav/.codex/generated_images/01a0857a-b230-7430-96e8-3efaf7bec95c/`. The final tanker is `exec-b6fc6570-bb0c-4392-8b58-b25889f20f27.png`; the plant is `exec-f9e4a219-eed4-4662-87a3-1483d2be50e7.png`. WebP copies use quality 88 at the original dimensions. ## Generation direction

Tanker edit: keep the same complete Indian Tata tanker, front-and-side three-quarter camera angle, silhouette, blue-white cab, garland, decorated cab signage, silver tank, red-yellow stripes, wheels, markings and lighting. Remove all scenery, road and external shadow. Place it on uniform charcoal with small margins; preserve opaque reflective glazing. Do not convert it to a straight side profile, crop its tyres or add branding.

Plant edit: preserve the exact low front-and-side viewpoint of the supplied header. Remove the tanker and reconstruct the facility, fence, kerb and road behind it. Extend the image horizontally while preserving camera height and direction. The road recedes toward the left; industrial structures show depth, with vertical columns and diagonal pipe racks. Keep daylight, steel columns, blue supports and yellow rails. Exclude vehicles, people, text and signage.

## Motion and maintenance

`HeroRoadScene` owns visibility, pause and resize handling. CSS moves the scenery and kerb markings at 32 pixels per second and the road texture and lane markings at 144 pixels per second. Wheel duration equals the circumference of the rendered front tyre divided by road speed. The vehicle and axle centres stay fixed while scenery travels past them. Rim lighting remains stationary; only symmetrical vents and lug details rotate, avoiding the wobble caused by rotating photographic highlights.

Clip the lower 33.8% of each levelled plant panel, preserving its original image dimensions. This removes the source kerb and road, which vary in height. Anchor the continuous CSS kerb at that crop line with a one-pixel overlap. Extend the road down 900 pixels from that fixed top edge so its lower boundary never enters the hero, including on mobile. Keep the road colour uniform; apply the footer shading once over the complete scene.

The animation pauses offscreen and in hidden tabs. The motion button pauses every layer without resetting its position. Reduced motion displays a complete still scene. The homepage heading and links remain server-rendered and usable without animation.

Changing the tanker image requires updating its SVG silhouette, wheel centers, elliptical rim dimensions and front-tyre diameter ratio. Replacing the plant requires checking repeat joins and the crop above its original kerb at desktop and mobile widths.
